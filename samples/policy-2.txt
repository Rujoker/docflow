Privacy Policy — Quietnote
Last updated: 15 August 2026

1. What we collect
Quietnote stores the notes you write and the email address you sign up with. Nothing else.
Notes are encrypted on your device before they are uploaded.

2. How we use your data
Your email address is used only to sign you in and to send security notices.
We do not read your notes and we do not use them to train any models.

3. Sharing
We do not sell your data.
Payments are handled by our payment processor, which receives only what it needs to charge your card; it is the only third party that processes your data.

4. Retention
When you delete your account, your notes and email address are erased within 30 days.

5. Your rights
You can export or delete all your data from the Settings page at any time.

6. Changes
We will email you 30 days before any change to this policy takes effect.

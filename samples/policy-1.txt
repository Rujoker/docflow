Privacy Policy — Brightloop Fitness App
Last updated: 1 September 2026

1. What we collect
We collect your name, email address, date of birth, height, weight, and workout history.
When you connect a wearable device, we also collect heart rate, sleep and step data.
We collect your approximate location every time you open the app.

2. How we use your data
We use your data to build personalised training plans and to improve the app.
We may use your workout history to train our recommendation models.

3. Sharing with third parties
We share your email address and age range with advertising partners and other third parties.
Aggregated health statistics may be sold to third parties for research and marketing purposes.
We may disclose your location history to analytics providers and other third parties.
If Brightloop is acquired, your data will be transferred to the new owner without further notice.

4. Retention
We keep your data for as long as your account exists and for 5 years after it is closed.

5. Your rights
You can request a copy of your data by writing to privacy@brightloop.example.
Account deletion requests are processed within 90 days.

6. Changes
We may change this policy at any time. Continued use of the app means you accept the changes.
